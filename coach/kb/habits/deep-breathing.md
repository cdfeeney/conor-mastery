Physiological sigh: double inhale through the nose, long exhale through the mouth, 1-3 reps (rBdhqBGqiMc @ 02:09).
Long pursed-lip exhales slow the heart rate (KHIbgSN2qAU @ 02:05). In a 1-month RCT, 5 min/day of cyclic sighing improved mood more than mindfulness meditation (res:balban2023), so the app paces cyclic sighing.
