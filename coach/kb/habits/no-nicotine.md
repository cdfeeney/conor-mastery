why: "Unaided cold turkey works only about 5% of the time, and most relapses happen in the first week, so plan for that week instead of relying on willpower. In a 697-person trial, quitting abruptly on a set quit day beat cutting down gradually (49% vs 39% quit at 4 weeks). Part of the pull is the ritual of putting a pouch in, so replace the ritual with a non-nicotine swap. Morning urges are strongest because you wake in withdrawal. Withdrawal peaks on days 2-3 and the physical part fades over 3-4 weeks. Support plus medication is the top-rated (grade A) approach, and a patch combined with gum or lozenges works better than either alone. Zyn itself is not an approved quit aid, so talk to a doctor about a plan. The app does not give doses."
