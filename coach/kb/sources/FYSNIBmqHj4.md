## Claims
- Morning light on the eyes triggers a cortisol spike and sets a sleep timer; melatonin arrives about 16 hours later. [00:30]
- Hours indoors on the phone after waking delay the signal, pushing sleepiness later. [00:30]
- 10-15 minutes outside, no staring at the sun, is the suggested dose. [01:01]
- A sugary breakfast spikes then crashes blood sugar; a protein-first first meal keeps energy steadier and fullness longer. [01:32]
- Cold showers raise dopamine and norepinephrine for hours, per the creator. [02:34]
- Ten push-ups each morning serve as a small "vote" for an identity of someone who looks after their body. [02:34]
- Scrolling spikes dopamine and, the creator argues, makes effortful tasks feel duller afterward. [03:36]
- Not scrolling in the morning is the fifth habit. [04:06]

## Protocol candidates
- 10-15 min outdoors soon after waking. [01:01]
- Make the first meal protein-heavy. [01:32]
- 10 push-ups every morning. [02:34]
- No scrolling in the morning. [04:06]
- Optional: finish with a cold shower. [02:34]
