## Claims
- Low motivation is reframed as low biological energy, so fix energy with tiny habits first. [01:38]
- Self-criticism about the past blocks change; forgive past self and act on what is controllable. [01:02]
- Basics that restore energy: water in sight on the desk, regular sleep, cutting sugary drinks and ultra-processed food, light movement. [02:15]
- Motivation follows action, not the reverse. [04:29]
- Two-minute rule: commit to only a tiny slice of the task, shrinking it until you start. [04:29]
- Five-second rule: count down from five and just do it before the brain argues. [05:05]
- Ten-minute rule: when a craving hits, set a 10-minute timer and do something else; re-set if the urge remains. [06:12]
- Scale the rules up gradually as they get easy. [06:46]
## Protocol candidates
- workout: If resistance shows, count 5-4-3-2-1 and start; commit to two minutes only. [05:05]
- no-nicotine: On a Zyn urge, set a 10-minute timer and leave the room or move; repeat if needed. [06:12]
- no-social-media: Same 10-minute delay before opening any feed. [06:12]
- sleep: Seven to nine hours, shifting bedtime earlier gradually. [02:49]
## Game-design notes
- 14-day challenge of small energy habits (water, sleep, diet, simple workout). [07:16]
