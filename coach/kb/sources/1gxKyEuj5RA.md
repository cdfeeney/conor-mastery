## Claims
- Pushing work onto "tomorrow you" just makes tomorrow worse. [00:00]
- Lever points are tiny decisions with outsized effect on the day. [00:32]
- Picking up the phone in the morning can derail the whole day. [00:32]
- Identify your lever points first, then track them as habits. [01:02]
- Environment design: make bad choices harder and good ones easier (no cake at home). [01:32]
- For morning scrolling: delete social apps at night, put a sticky note on the phone, or put a book where the phone goes. [02:03]
- When the environment cannot change, write the intended action in advance in a set format. [02:03]
- Pre-deciding removes morning decisions that otherwise cause inaction. [02:34]
- Only a little discipline at a few key moments is needed. [03:04]
