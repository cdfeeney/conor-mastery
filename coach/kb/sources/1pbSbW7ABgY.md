## Claims
- A realistic daily routine beats an extreme 4 am hero routine for people with work or school. [00:00]
- Avoid the phone for at least 30 minutes after waking; early dopamine flooding is claimed to hurt focus all day. [01:04]
- Morning sunlight or bright light, even on cloudy days, strengthens the healthy morning cortisol rise. [01:35]
- Without sun, use the brightest light available. [02:06]
- Exercise raises dopamine, norepinephrine and serotonin and also BDNF, helping mood and learning for hours. [02:06]
- Suggested: 10-15 min cardio outside; consistency matters more than amount. [02:37]
- A cold shower after the workout gives a bigger, longer norepinephrine/dopamine lift. [03:07]
- Each disciplined morning act acts as a "vote" for being disciplined, making later hard tasks easier. [03:37]
- Make breakfast about 80% protein (eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, chicken), with a little carbs and fat. [04:09]

## Protocol candidates
- No phone for the first 30 min after waking. [01:04]
- Go outside for light, then 10-15 min cardio. [02:37]
- Finish with a cold shower, then protein breakfast. [03:07]

## Evidence notes
- Opinion with simplified neuroscience, no citations; the "80% protein" figure and the claim that discipline carries over are his own framing. [03:37]
- Cold exposure and intense exercise: my caution, check with a doctor if you have heart or blood-pressure conditions.
- Nothing about medication or supplements.
