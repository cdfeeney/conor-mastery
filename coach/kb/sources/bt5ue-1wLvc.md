## Claims
- Going to bed and waking at the same times daily, plus morning sunlight, anchors the circadian rhythm. [01:01]
- Falling asleep requires core temperature to drop; a too-warm room keeps you awake. [01:31]
- A bath or hot shower before bed draws blood to the skin so the body cools faster; hands and feet out of the blanket also help. [01:31]
- Scrolling in bed is presented as the worst sleep habit, with blue light hurting melatonin. [02:01]
- Quitting scrolling cold turkey likely fails; step down: longer videos, then a movie, then audiobook or podcast. [02:01]
- Use phone downtime settings to block social apps near bedtime, with a friend holding the passcode. [02:31]
- Dim lights an hour before bed and avoid laptop/phone screens; tea, tidying, journaling, or an audiobook instead. [03:01]
- Coffee and tea linger in the body; set a caffeine cutoff such as 2 pm. [03:32]

## Protocol candidates
- Fixed bedtime and wake time, plus morning outdoor light. [01:01]
- Lights low and screens off from one hour before bed. [03:01]
- Replace bedtime scrolling with an audiobook or podcast; set app downtime and a friend-held passcode. [02:31]
- No caffeine after 2 pm. [03:32]
- Cool room; hot shower before bed. [01:31]
