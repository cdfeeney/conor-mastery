## Claims
- Better focus shortens required study time. [00:30]
- Make the first steps so easy that starting is nearly automatic ("levels"), since 8 hours straight feels impossible. [01:01]
- Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors, raising alertness. [01:01]
- Hydration and a protein breakfast support steady focus (stated, no source). [01:33]
- Remove distractions before sitting down: tidy desk, close tabs, block sites, phone off and outside. [01:33]
- Motivation arrives after starting; begin with 10 minutes on a favorite subject. [02:04]
- An AI-made plan reduces overwhelm. [02:34]
- Work in 25-90 minute blocks with breaks, no distractions inside blocks. [02:34]
- A screen break (TikTok) spikes dopamine and kills later motivation; walk or move, no screens. [03:05]
- Meditation, about 10 minutes daily, is claimed to improve working memory and distraction resistance. [04:06]
## Protocol candidates
- Pre-focus checklist: phone outside, tabs closed, sites blocked, water at desk. [01:33]
- 10-minute easy start on a favorite subject. [02:04]
- Timer block, then a screen-free movement break. [03:05]
## Game-design notes
- Treat focus as a level ladder where level one is trivially easy to start. [01:01]
## Evidence notes
- Adenosine and dopamine explanations presented as science but unsourced. Meditation "scientific paper" claim uncited. Remainder opinion.
