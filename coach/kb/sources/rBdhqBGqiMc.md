## Claims
- Most stress tools require stepping away; the physiological sigh calms you in real time, mid-task. [00:35]
- The pattern is two inhales followed by one long, extended exhale. [00:35]
- People do it spontaneously when carbon dioxide builds up (deep sleep, cramped spaces); you can do it on purpose. [01:06]
- Inhale through the nose (first inhale longer, second shorter), exhale through the mouth. [01:06]
- The second inhale reinflates collapsed air sacs in the lungs, so the exhale offloads more carbon dioxide. [01:38]
- One to three sighs are usually enough to bring stress and alertness down quickly. [02:09]
## Protocol candidates
- When stress spikes: double inhale through the nose, long exhale through the mouth, 1-3 times. [02:09]
- Use as the 1-minute minimum of the breathing habit. [02:09]
