## Claims
- Feeling lost around 20-30 is partly the loss of externally imposed structure. [01:31]
- Build structure first; action creates motivation rather than the reverse. [02:02]
- 3-5-1 system over 90 days: three habits, five rules, one project. [01:01]
- Pick one habit each for body, mind, output; limit to three because willpower is finite. [02:02]
- Write habits down, keep them visible, and do not change them for 90 days. [02:33]
- Start tiny (five push-ups, two-minute meditation) and raise difficulty only once consistent. [02:33]
- Rules are standards you live by, distinct from habits you do. [03:03]
- Sample rules: no phone first 30 minutes, under 2h social media, never skip two days in a row. [03:34]
- Choose one project to actually create for 90 days; refuse new ideas meanwhile. [04:34]
- New ideas always look more exciting than the current project; stay put. [05:05]
- Close the decide-to-do gap: write the plan down, put it on lock screen or paper, do one habit for two minutes tomorrow. [05:36]
## Protocol candidates
- 90-day lock: three habits fixed, no swapping. [02:33]
- Rules list of five shown next to habits. [03:34]
- Day 1: one habit for two minutes; day 3 add second; week 2 all three. [05:36]
- Show habits/rules on the lock screen. [05:36]
