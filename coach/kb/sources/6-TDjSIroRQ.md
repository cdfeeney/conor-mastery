## Claims
- Re-reading and highlighting feel familiar but do not store anything (fluency illusion). [00:34]
- Active recall: close the book and write everything remembered on blank paper, then check gaps. [01:04]
- Spaced repetition: review just before forgetting, using flashcards with review dates. [01:37]
- Attention runs in roughly 90-minute alert blocks followed by about 20 minutes of rest. [02:08]
- During rest, avoid screens: walk, meditate or stare out the window. [02:41]
- Cutting sleep after studying loses the memory-consolidation window. [03:13]
## Protocol candidates
- reset-focus: Run one 90-minute single-task block with a timer; stop when focus fades. [02:08]
- reset-focus: After the block, take a 20-minute screen-free break. [02:41]
- sleep: Protect sleep the night after learning; do not cram late. [03:13]
