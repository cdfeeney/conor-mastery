## Claims
- Dopamine tracks surprise versus expectation, not reward itself; a worse-than-expected outcome drops it below baseline [02:12]
- Porn, scrolling and nicotine give far bigger learning signals than natural rewards, so the brain learns to chase them [04:19]
- Constant exposure raises the baseline, so ordinary rewards (learning, sport) start to feel disappointing [05:21]
- The cycle of locking in then falling off is blamed on this dopamine reset [05:56]
- He says the tactics are already known (sleep, exercise, diet, quit social media, add friction); the missing piece is real motivation [06:28]
- Suggested fix: picture in detail where life goes if nothing changes, to create enough pain to commit [07:02]
## Protocol candidates
- no-social-media: once, take an hour with all tech off, pen and paper, and write what your life looks like in a few years if you keep going as now [07:33]
- no-nicotine: in the same session, list what nicotine costs you and what you lose if it continues [07:02]
