What we took from Monke

MonkeConor Mastery
BananasGrit. Hard habits pay 15, medium 10, easy 5, and the bad-day minimum pays half. It buys garden items, land, and a 15-minute app unlock.
Lvl + 1 XP/focus minBelt ranks from white to black. You earn 10 XP per habit, 1 XP per focus minute, and 10 for an on-target night.
Streak + potionsPerfect Day streak. Seven perfect days earn a Second Wind that saves one miss. You can hold two at most, and you can't buy them.
Island you buildA dojo garden that grows from a fire pit to a pagoda, using original art only.
Hardcore focusLocked in: your apps are blocked, you earn 1.5x grit, and stopping early costs 10.
Lock apps until habits doneMorning gate: social apps lock at 21:30 and stay locked until your morning routine is done.
Random daily challengeThe coach picks the challenge from your weakest habit over the last 14 days.

Where the coach's advice comes from

Each habit's "why", identity line and daily challenges are compiled from 12 YouTube source notes. The sources include Huberman, Matthew Walker, James Clear and the monke channel itself, plus Franklin's virtue tracking, and every timestamp citation was checked against its transcript by a script. Tap a habit in Coach to see its sources.
